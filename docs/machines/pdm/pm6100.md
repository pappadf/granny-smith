# Power Macintosh 6100/60 (`pm6100`)

The first PowerPC Macintosh ("PDM"/"Piltdown Man", March 1994): 60 MHz
MPC601, 30 MHz bus (2:1), machine-ID register `$A55A3010`.  One SIMM-pair
socket: its two banks decode at `$10000000`/`$08000000` at power-on and
are relocated contiguously after the 8 MB soldered bank by the HMC
SIMM_BANK_SIZE config write (hmc.md).  Single PDS slot; without the NuBus
adapter the whole BART space bus-errors, which the base model's probe
expects.  RAM options 8–72 MB (default 24 MB — the config that exercises
the bank relocation).

See pdm.md for the family model and the boot-ladder status.
