# Apple Macintosh II Video Card

> 🚧 **Placeholder** — this page is planned but not yet written.
<!-- gs-doc-status: stub -->

The Apple Macintosh II Video Card (630-0153, codename "Toby") is the NuBus
video card the Macintosh II shipped with in 1987: a fixed 640 × 480 raster at
1, 2, 4 or 8 bits per pixel (8 bpp needs the 512 KB expansion kit), drawn by
the TFB chip through a Bt453 RAMDAC, with a 4 KB declaration ROM (342-0008).
