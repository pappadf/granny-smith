The Macintosh IIfx (1990): Apple's high-end 40 MHz 68030 machine, set apart from the II family by a cluster of custom support chips — I/O Processors, a dedicated interrupt controller and an Apple SCSI DMA chip. A family of one: this page carries the family role (see §1).
