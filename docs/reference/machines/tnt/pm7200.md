# Power Macintosh 7200

The Power Macintosh 7200 (August 1995; 7200/75 and 7200/90, later a 7200/120) is the entry model of
the PCI generation and the one machine of Apple's "Catalyst" board. It shares the TNT family's
boot ROM, Bandit PCI bridge, Grand Central I/O controller, Curio, Cuda, AWACS and SWIM III and
the whole DBDMA and interrupt architecture ([tnt.md](tnt.md) §6.4), but its memory controller is
**Platinum** ([platinum.md](platinum.md)), which is also its video controller, and it has no
processor card, no Chaos display bus, no Control and no MESH. This page carries the machine's
deltas against the family page.

## 1. Identity

### 1.1 Identity values

| Value | 7200 | Source |

| `gestaltMachineType` | 108 | [1] §"Machine Identification" |
| BoxFlag ($0CB3) | $66 | the Catalyst ProductInfo record [2] |
| ROM | the 7500/8500/9500 ROM, `$96CD923D` or `$9630C68B` | [2] |
| $F8000000 identifier | upper halfword $3001 (Platinum) | [2]; [platinum.md](platinum.md) §4.3 |
| BoxID ($F301A000, little-endian) | bit 15 high, bit 14 low (no MESH), bit 8 high (the factory-test strap's idle level) | [2] (*inferred* from the family's straps; no 7200 board was read) |
| Open Firmware `compatible` | the Catalyst arm (`AAPL,7300`, then `MacRISC`) — the image has no `AAPL,7200` string | [2]; [hammerhead.md](hammerhead.md) §4.3 |

The ROM, not a separate image, identifies the board: the 68k identification reads the first byte
of $F8000000 ($39 on a Hammerhead), and a part whose identifier's upper halfword is $3001 takes
the Catalyst ProductInfo, with its own `DecoderInfo` (no MESH entry) [2]. System 7.5.x, 7.6 and
Mac OS 8.1 print the generic "Power Macintosh" / "Mac OS computer" in About This Macintosh /
About This Computer.

## 2. Deltas vs the family doc

1. **Platinum at $F8000000** in place of Hammerhead: a different register file — eight bank base
   registers at +$060..+$0D0 holding base addresses, linear organisation only, no interleaving —
   and the display registers ([platinum.md](platinum.md) §2).
2. **Video is Platinum's**, on the processor bus: frame buffer at $F1000000, DACula at Grand
   Central +$1B000, retrace on Grand Central line 30 (not 26). There is no Chaos bridge at
   $F0000000 and no VCI bus.
3. **No MESH**: island +$18000 is unused and DBDMA channel 10 idle ([dbdma.md](dbdma.md)); the 53C94
   is the only SCSI controller and carries the internal hard disk, the CD-ROM drive and the
   external connector on one bus.
4. **No processor card**: the 601 is on the logic board; the processor bus runs at half the CPU
   clock.
5. **Four DIMM slots** (eight banks), 256 MB, against the 7500's eight slots.
6. **No AV**: no video input, no DAV connector, no second-stream output [1] Table 1-1.

## 3. Per-subsystem wiring

### 3.1 Memory

Bank k of the eight answers at the address in its bank base register; POST probes each bank in a
128 MB window and re-bases the populated ones contiguously from 0 ([platinum.md](platinum.md)
§4.1). A DIMM is one or two banks of 4–128 MB.

### 3.2 Video

The 13" RGB (640×480 at ≈67 Hz) at 8 or 16 bpp on the soldered 1 MB; the 21" (1152×870) at 8 bpp;
the portrait display (640×870) at 8 bpp; the 12" (512×384) up to 32 bpp [1] Table 2-1. Mac OS's
default on the 13" strap with 1 MB is 640×480 at 16 bpp.

### 3.3 SCSI

The Curio 53C94 at Grand Central +$10000 with DBDMA channel 0, interrupt 12 — the family's
external cell ([grand-central.md](grand-central.md) §2.5) — is the startup bus. The ROM's SCSI
Manager scans it for the startup disk; the internal end is terminated by the internal hard disk
[1] §"SCSI Port".

### 3.4 Everything else

Cuda/ADB, the ESCC, SWIM III, AWACS, MACE, the NVRAM and the interrupt block are the family's,
unchanged ([tnt.md](tnt.md) §4.3).

## 4. Expansion

- **PCI**: three sockets on Bandit at IDSEL 13/14/15, interrupts on Grand Central externals
  23/24/25 (the 7500's assignment, *inferred*; the 7200 developer note gives no table).
- **DRAM**: four DIMM slots, any size in any slot [1] §"RAM DIMMs".
- **VRAM**: three VRAM DIMM slots, total 2 or 4 MB [1] §"VRAM and VRAM Expansion".
- **L2**: one cache DIMM slot.

## 5. Boot sequence summary

The family's boot contract ([tnt.md](tnt.md) §2.7) with the Catalyst branch points: POST sizes
DRAM through Platinum's bank registers; Open Firmware builds the `platinum` display node and no
`chaos`, `control` or `mesh`; the 68k identification selects the Catalyst ProductInfo (BoxFlag
$66); the ROM's `.Display_Video_Apple_Platinum` driver sizes VRAM, reads the sense lines and sets
the mode ([platinum.md](platinum.md) §4); the startup disk is found on the 53C94 bus. System 7.5.3,
System 7.6 and Mac OS 8.1 start from the 53C94 disk, and the System 7.6 "Install 1" floppy starts
to the Installer (System 7.5.3 and Mac OS 8.1 observed on `$96CD923D`, System 7.6 on
`$9630C68B`) [2].
