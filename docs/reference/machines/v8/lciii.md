# Macintosh LC III

> 🚧 **Placeholder** — this page is planned but not yet written.
<!-- gs-doc-status: stub -->

The LC II's slim-line case around the Sonora decoder, a 25 MHz 68030, 4 MB soldered plus one 72-pin SIMM socket (36 MB at most), 512 KB of VRAM and an external monitor port (1993; also the Performa 450) [1] ch. 1. The 33 MHz LC III+ (Performa 460/466/467) is the same board with a faster clock. The ROM identifies both by the CPU ID register at `$5FFFFFFC`. The family-level facts are in [v8.md](v8.md).
