# The V8 family — Macintosh LC, LC II, Classic II, Color Classic, and the Sonora boards

> 🚧 **Placeholder** — this page is planned but not yet written.
<!-- gs-doc-status: stub -->

Apple's low-cost 68020/68030 boards of 1990-1993, each built around one
custom decoder that integrates the functions the Macintosh IIci spread over
the MDU, the RBV, VIA2 and the Apple Sound Chip: the **V8** (Macintosh LC,
LC II), the **Eagle** (Classic II) and the **Spice** (Color Classic) [1]
p. 5, [3] p. 9, [4] ch. 2.  What follows is the outline the full page will
fill; the facts already gathered are stated with their sources.

## 1. Overview & membership

| Machine | CPU | Decoder | Soldered RAM | Video | Companion |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Macintosh LC | 68020, 15.6672 MHz | V8 | 2 MB | external, VRAM SIMM | ADB microcontroller [1] |
| Macintosh LC II | 68030, 15.6672 MHz | V8 | 4 MB | external, VRAM SIMM | ADB microcontroller [2] |
| Macintosh Classic II | 68030, 15.6672 MHz | Eagle | 2 MB | built-in 512 × 342 × 1, DRAM | ADB microcontroller [3] |
| Macintosh Color Classic | 68030, 15.6672 MHz | Spice | 4 MB | built-in 512 × 384, VRAM | Cuda (the Developer Note's "ADB microcontroller"; soft power) [4] |
| Macintosh LC III / LC III+ | 68030, 25 / 33 MHz | Sonora | 4 MB | external, 512 KB VRAM | ADB microcontroller [5] |
| Macintosh LC 520 / LC 550 | 68030, 25 / 33 MHz | Ardbeg (Sonora) | 4 MB | built-in 640 × 480, VRAM | Cuda [6] |
| Macintosh Color Classic II | 68030, 33 MHz | Sonora | 4 MB | built-in 512 × 384, VRAM | Cuda |

The Sonora "provides all the functionality of the V8 gate array" and adds
SWIM2, more video modes and a 32-bit-only address decoder [5] p. 3, p. 7:
RAM from 0 (up to 36 MB), ROM at `$40800000`, I/O at `$50F00000`, VRAM at
`$60B00000`, the PDS at `$FE000000` [5] Table 1-1.  The 24-bit map is the
PMMU's remapping.  Expansion RAM starts at `$400000`, directly above the
soldered 4 MB [5] Table 1-2.

## 2. Board architecture common to the family

The decoder provides "timing, video generation, memory mapping, sound,
clock generation" and the glue functions, a full VIA1, and "a pseudo-VIA2
that consists of registers similar to those implemented in the RBV" [1]
p. 11, [3] p. 9.  The SCC and the 53C80 are one Combo chip [1] p. 7.

## 3. Memory map & address decode shared by the family

RAM occupies the first 10 MB; ROM `$40A00000` (24-bit `$A00000`); the I/O
space `$50F00000` (24-bit `$F00000`); VRAM `$50F40000-$50FBFFFF`; the PDS
card answers as slot `$E` [1] Table 1-1.  The same low addresses serve in
either addressing mode [3] p. 9.  The decoder's expansion register holds
three RAM size bits written at power-on [1] p. 12; the top 2 MB of soldered
RAM always appears in the top 2 MB of the 10 MB space [4] pp. 6, 22.  The
power-up overlay maps ROM at zero until the first access to the ROM range
[1] p. 12.
